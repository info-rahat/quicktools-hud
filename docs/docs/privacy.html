Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Summary: QuickTools HUD does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

QuickTools HUD does not collect any personal information. The application operates entirely on your local device. Specifically:

  • No account registration or login is required.
  • No analytics or telemetry data is collected.
  • No usage statistics are sent to any server.
  • No crash reports are transmitted automatically.

2. Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored only on your device in your local AppData folder:

  • Clipboard History — text and image snippets you have copied, if the Clipboard tool is used.
  • Tool Slot Configuration — your custom tool layout preferences.
  • Screen Recordings — saved to your Videos folder only when you initiate a recording.
  • Screenshots — saved to your Pictures folder only when you take a screenshot.

This data never leaves your device and is fully under your control. You can delete it at any time.

3. Permissions Used

  • Screen Capture — required for Screenshot and Screen Recorder tools.
  • Microphone — optional, only used during screen recording if you enable audio.
  • Clipboard Access — required for Clipboard History tool.
  • File System — to save screenshots and recordings to your device.

No permission is used for any purpose other than the tool it enables.

4. Third-Party Services

QuickTools HUD does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or advertising networks. No data is shared with any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

QuickTools HUD does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
