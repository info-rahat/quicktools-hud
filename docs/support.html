Frequently Asked Questions
How do I open the HUD?
Press PrtSc (Print Screen) on your keyboard from anywhere.
The HUD doesn't open when I press PrtSc
Make sure QuickTools HUD is running in the system tray (bottom-right of taskbar). Click the ^ arrow to find hidden tray icons.
How do I change the tool in a slot?
Click the ✎ Edit button in the top-right corner of the HUD, then click any slot to change it.
Where are recordings saved?
Recordings are saved to Videos\QuickTools Recordings in your user folder.
How do I exit the app completely?
Right-click the QuickTools HUD icon in the system tray and select Exit.
